## Notes

- **FPS/W_delta is the primary efficiency metric** for this paper. It uses only the power attributable to inference (active minus idle), removing fixed OS/Jupyter overhead (~4.83 W). This is more conservative and defensible than FPS/W_total.
- **Data quality is excellent** — FPS std dev is <0.1% across 10 rounds for all DPU runs, and power std dev is <2%. Results are highly reproducible.
- **DPU_CU_delta column shows N/A throughout** — xbutil CU invocation counter was not returning values during the run (likely a timing/permission issue with xbutil examine under load). This does not affect any other measurement.
- **Latency spikes in ResNet50 CPU rounds 1 and 9** (max 1657ms, 1820ms vs typical ~640ms) — single-frame OS scheduling jitter. Mean latency is unaffected across 100 frames. The raw per-frame latency CSVs in `raw_latencies/` show this clearly.
- **YOLOv3 CPU latency std dev** is high (80±107ms) due to occasional scheduling jitter on the ~4.6s per-frame runs. The median is stable.
- **CPU governor is `userspace` at fixed 1333 MHz** — no thermal throttling occurred. All 4 cores ran at max frequency throughout all CPU runs.
- **CMA free memory** dropped gradually across rounds (856 MB → 711 MB for YOLOv3) due to DPU memory not being fully released between rounds. Remained well above the 500 MB safety threshold throughout.
